# Privacy Policy for Job Hunt Focus

_Last updated: November 17, 2026_

Job Hunt Focus does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

## What the extension stores

Everything the extension saves stays in your browser's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`) on your own device:

- your to-do items, including the title and address of job pages you choose to save
- saved tab sessions (the addresses of tabs in a window you choose to save)
- your settings: site lists, timer durations and daily goal
- a count of completed to-dos per day, used for the daily goal and streak

None of this data is sent to the developer or to any other server. The extension makes no network requests of its own and contains no analytics or tracking.

## Permissions

- **tabs**: reads the title and address of the current tab when you save a job or a tab session.
- **declarativeNetRequest** and **host access to all sites**: redirect blocked sites to the extension's focus page during a focus session. Pages are matched by domain only; their content is never read.
- **storage**: saves the data listed above on your device.
- **alarms** and **notifications**: run the focus timer and tell you when a session or break ends.
- **contextMenus**: adds "Save job as todo" to the right-click menu.

## Deleting your data

Remove items in the extension at any time. Uninstalling the extension deletes all of its stored data.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
